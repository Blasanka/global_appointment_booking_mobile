DESCRIPTION
SalonFlow helps salon owners and staff manage daily operations in one clean workspace.

Use SalonFlow to:
- manage appointments with day, week, and month calendar views
- track clients, visit history, and contact details
- organize services, staff, and working hours
- review revenue, top services, and staff activity
- store salon profile details and business settings
- launch WhatsApp and call actions from client records
- keep notifications, templates, and subscription details in one place

Built for salon teams that want a simple, organized way to handle bookings and client management without spreadsheet or notebook chaos.

Key features:
- appointment dashboard with upcoming bookings
- calendar navigation across day, week, and month views
- client directory with history and quick contact actions
- staff and service management
- reports for revenue and top-performing services
- settings for salon profile, working hours, staff accounts, templates, and privacy information

SalonFlow is designed for salon operations such as appointment tracking, staff coordination, customer management, and business reporting.
