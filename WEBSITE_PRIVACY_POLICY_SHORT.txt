1. WHAT WE COLLECT

SalonFlow may handle:
- account details such as name, email, phone number, and login information
- salon business details such as salon name, address, staff records, services, working hours, and settings

- operational app data used to support bookings, reports, notifications, and templates

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to:
- operate the app and its booking, client, staff, and reporting features

3. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information:
- with service providers who help operate the app
- when required by law or legal process

- with external apps only when the user explicitly starts a call, WhatsApp message, or browser action
