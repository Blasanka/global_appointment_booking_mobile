SalonFlow ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how SalonFlow collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SalonFlow mobile application and related services.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect and store the following types of information:

- Account information, such as name, email address, phone number, and login credentials
- Business information, such as salon name, address, working hours, staff details, service lists, pricing, and subscription details
- Client management information, such as client names, phone numbers, visit history, appointment history, service history, and notes entered by the salon
- Operational usage data needed to display bookings, reports, notifications, templates, and settings
- Device or app interaction data if analytics, crash reporting, or other diagnostics tools are enabled in a production release

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to:

- provide appointment, client, staff, reporting, and settings features
- manage salon operations within the app
- enable user-requested contact actions such as phone calls or WhatsApp actions
- improve product quality, stability, and usability
- provide support, service communications, and account-related updates
- meet legal, security, and compliance obligations

3. HOW INFORMATION IS SHARED

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only in the following cases:

- with service providers who help us operate the app, if such services are added in a production environment
- when required by law, legal process, or valid governmental request
- to protect users, the service, legal rights, or security
- with external apps only when the user explicitly launches actions such as a phone call, WhatsApp message, or web link

4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. DATA RETENTION
